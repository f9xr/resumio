Frequently asked questions

Is Resumio really free?

Yes. Resumio is completely free. Build, score and download your resume with no account, no watermark and no hidden fees.

What is an ATS score?

An ATS (Applicant Tracking System) score estimates how well your resume passes the software companies use to screen applications. Resumio scores your contact details, summary, quantified achievements, skills and formatting, then tells you exactly what to fix.

Does Resumio store my personal data?

No. Resumio runs entirely in your browser. Your resume is saved locally on your own device and is never uploaded to a server.

Can I download my resume as a PDF?

Yes. Use the Export PDF button to print or save your resume as a clean, ATS-friendly A4 PDF.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required. Open Resumio, start typing, and your progress is auto-saved in your browser.
